Short Bio

DJ Holly Weed is a Las Vegas DJ and professional circus performer spinning drum & bass, heavy bass, metal and hardcore. After years performing for thousands at MGM Grand, EDC's main stage and AREA15, she brought the show to the decks — three-hour sets at Southwest Flow Festival and a build-to-burn summer with Camp VIP at Burning Man. A performance, not just a set.

Long Bio

DJ Holly Weed came to the decks from the circus stage — years performing for crowds of thousands at MGM Grand, EDC's main stage and AREA15, sharing stages with RÜFÜS DU SOL, Marshmello and Desert Dwellers. She booked Burning Man before she'd played a single set, then delivered: three-hour sets at Southwest Flow Festival and a build-to-burn summer with Camp VIP (Very Inappropriate People) at Burning Man 2026. Her sets splice drum & bass and heavy low end with the rock, metal and hardcore she grew up on, with circus surprises built in. Up next: opening Pachi Pachi in downtown Las Vegas (November 13) and Dystopia in Austin with Dragonwolf Productions (January 16, 2027). Anything you can do, she can do higher.
