Las Vegas. Woke up one day and decided to DJ and haven't stopped since. Drum and bass and heavy low end spliced with the rock, metal and hardcore I actually grew up on — pit energy on a festival system.

New to DJing — not new to a stage. I've spent years as a professional circus performer, in front of thousands at MGM Grand, EDC's main stage and AREA15, sharing stages with RÜFÜS DU SOL, Marshmello and Desert Dwellers. The decks are the next evolution of what I bring to a stage, and they slot straight into the show I already do.

So book a performance, not a set. Expect circus surprises. No lane, no permission slip, no interest in playing it safe. I set every goal and I hit every one of them. Try and stop me.
