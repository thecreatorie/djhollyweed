01 — About

Circus stage,
meet the decks.

DJ Holly Weed came to the decks from the circus stage — years performing for crowds of thousands at MGM Grand, EDC's main stage and AREA15, sharing stages with RÜFÜS DU SOL, Marshmello and Desert Dwellers. She booked Burning Man before she'd played a single set, then delivered: three separate one-hour sets at Southwest Flow Festival, sets during build and tear down with Camp VIP at Burning Man 2026, and an opening set at PULSE at Taverna Costera in the Las Vegas Arts District. Expect circus surprises.
